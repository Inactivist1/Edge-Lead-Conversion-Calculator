Wind: full-value crosswind. Blowing the same way the target moves means less lead; blowing against it means more. Trigger lag: 0 for ambush; about 0.05 s if you track.

    
Pressure is station pressure, what a Kestrel reads where you stand, not the sea-level number on a weather report. Typing an altitude resets pressure to standard for that altitude.
