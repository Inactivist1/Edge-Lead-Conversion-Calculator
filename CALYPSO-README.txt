MOVER LEAD - LIVE WIND FROM A CALYPSO METER

Mover Lead can read wind straight from a Calypso ultrasonic wind meter over
Bluetooth. It turns the reading into the crosswind across your line of fire and
fills in the Wind speed and Wind direction (L -> R / R -> L) fields for you, so
your hold updates as the wind changes.

Built from Calypso's published Bluetooth format for the Ultrasonic Portable
(UP10). Other Calypso models that use the same format should work too.


WHAT YOU NEED
 - A Calypso ultrasonic wind meter, charged and switched on.
 - iPhone: the free Bluefy browser from the App Store. Safari can't use
   Bluetooth, so the Calypso feature only works when Mover Lead is opened
   in Bluefy.
   https://apps.apple.com/app/id1492822055
 - Android: Chrome. Nothing extra to install.


ONE-TIME SETUP (iPhone)
 1. Install Bluefy.
 2. Open Mover Lead in Safari, scroll to Conditions and Shooter, and tap
    COPY THIS LINK in the Calypso wind meter box.
    (Or type the address: https://inactivist1.github.io/Edge-Lead-Conversion-Calculator/ )
 3. Open Bluefy, paste the link in the address bar, and open it.
 4. Save the page as a favorite or bookmark in Bluefy.
 5. Enter your load and settings again in Bluefy. Bluefy and Safari keep
    separate settings.
 6. Optional home-screen button: open the Shortcuts app, tap +, add the
    "Open App" action, choose Bluefy, then Add to Home Screen. Name it
    something like "Mover Lead Live".

 Note: iPhone can't make a home-screen icon that opens a page directly in
 Bluefy. The button opens Bluefy; tap your saved Mover Lead page from there.


AT THE LINE
 1. Set the Calypso on the firing line with its ORIENTATION MARK POINTING AT
    THE TARGET (downrange). The app reads wind relative to that mark, so this
    matters.
 2. Open Mover Lead in Bluefy and tap CONNECT CALYPSO.
 3. Pick your meter from the list. It usually shows as "ULTRASONIC".
 4. Leave LIVE WIND ticked. The wind fields fill in and the hold updates
    about once a second.

 The readout looks like this:
   6.7 mph from 10 o'clock - crosswind 5.8 mph L -> R
   10-second average - battery 80% - filling wind fields

 - "from 10 o'clock" is where the wind is coming from. 12 o'clock is the
   target, 3 o'clock is your right.
 - "crosswind" is the part of the wind blowing across your line of fire. That
   is what goes into the wind field. Wind straight from 12 or 6 o'clock gives
   little or no crosswind.
 - The number is a 10-second average, so single gusts don't swing your hold.


TAKING OVER BY HAND
 - Typing a wind speed or tapping L -> R / R -> L turns Live wind off, so your
   entry sticks. The meter keeps showing its reading.
 - Tick LIVE WIND again to hand control back to the meter.
 - Tap DISCONNECT when you're done. If the meter drops off, the wind fields
   keep the last reading.


TROUBLESHOOTING
 - The box says Safari doesn't have Bluetooth: you're in Safari or the
   home-screen icon. Open Mover Lead in Bluefy instead.
 - The meter isn't in the list: make sure it's on, charged and within a few
   yards, and that no other app (such as Anemotracker) is connected to it.
   Close that app, then try again.
 - Won't connect, or keeps dropping: switch the meter off and on, then
   connect again.
 - Direction looks reversed (wind from your right shows L -> R): check that
   the orientation mark points at the target. If it does, the meter is
   reporting differently than expected. Open METER DETAILS, take a
   screenshot, and send it in so the app can be adjusted.
 - Battery shows orange: 20% or less. Calypso meters slow down or stop
   sending data when the battery is low.


GOOD TO KNOW
 - Live wind assumes a full-value crosswind at the line. Wind downrange can be
   different. Watch your mirage and impacts, and override when the meter and
   the target disagree.
 - The meter's temperature reading is not used. It reads high in the sun.
   Use Get conditions or type temperature in.
 - Kestrel meters can't connect. Kestrel's Bluetooth link is closed to outside
   apps. Read the Kestrel and type the numbers in. The pressure field takes
   Kestrel station pressure directly.
 - Holds are starting points. Confirm them on a real mover and write the
   confirmed numbers on your arm card.
