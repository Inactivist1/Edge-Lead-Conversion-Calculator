MOVER LEAD - LIVE WIND FROM A CALYPSO METER

Mover Lead can read wind straight from a Calypso ultrasonic wind meter over
Bluetooth. It turns the reading into the crosswind across your line of fire and
fills in the Wind speed and Wind direction (L -> R / R -> L) fields for you, so
your hold updates as the wind changes.

Built from Calypso's published Bluetooth format for the Ultrasonic Portable
(UP10). Other Calypso models that use the same format should work too.


WHAT YOU NEED
 - A Calypso ultrasonic wind meter, charged and switched on.
 - iPhone: the free Bluefy browser from the App Store. Safari can't use
   Bluetooth, so the Calypso feature only works when Mover Lead is opened
   in Bluefy.
   https://apps.apple.com/app/id1492822055
 - Android: Chrome. Nothing extra to install.


ONE-TIME SETUP (iPhone)
About 5 minutes. Do it at home with a signal.

 Mover Lead's address:  https://edgeleadconversioncalculator.vercel.app

 A. INSTALL BLUEFY
  1. Open the App Store, search for "Bluefy - Web BLE Browser" (by PNN Soft)
     and tap Get. It's free.
     Or open Mover Lead in Safari, scroll to Conditions and Shooter, and tap
     GET BLUEFY in the Calypso wind meter box.
  2. Open Bluefy once. If it asks for Bluetooth access, tap Allow. Mover Lead
     can't see the Calypso without it.
     (If you said no by mistake: Settings > Privacy & Security > Bluetooth >
     turn Bluefy on.)

 B. OPEN MOVER LEAD IN BLUEFY
  1. In Bluefy, tap the address bar at the top.
  2. Type or paste:  edgeleadconversioncalculator.vercel.app
     (In Safari, COPY THIS LINK in the Calypso box copies it for you.)
  3. Tap Go. Mover Lead opens, and the Calypso box now shows a
     CONNECT CALYPSO button instead of the "Safari doesn't have Bluetooth"
     message. That's how you know you're in the right place.
  4. If Bluefy asks to use your location, tap Allow. That's for the
     Get conditions button.

 C. SAVE IT IN BLUEFY
  1. While Mover Lead is open, open Bluefy's menu and add the page to your
     Favorites (bookmarks).
  2. Next time, open Bluefy and tap Mover Lead in your Favorites. Bluefy may
     also reopen the last page you had open, which skips this step.

 D. ENTER YOUR SETTINGS AGAIN
  Bluefy and Safari each keep their own copy of your settings. In Bluefy,
  enter your muzzle velocity, G7 BC, target size and anything else you've
  changed from the defaults. Bluefy remembers them from then on.

 E. MAKE A "MOVER LEAD LIVE" BUTTON ON YOUR HOME SCREEN
  Bluefy's own icon just says "Bluefy". This gives you a home-screen button
  with your own name and icon that opens Bluefy.
  1. Open the Shortcuts app (built into the iPhone; it's in the App Library
     if it isn't on your home screen).
  2. Tap + at the top right to make a new shortcut.
  3. Tap "Add Action" (or the search bar at the bottom), type "Open App", and
     tap Open App.
  4. In the action, tap the word "App" and pick Bluefy from the list.
  5. Rename it: tap the shortcut's name at the top of the screen (it starts as
     "Open App" or "New Shortcut"), tap Rename, type
        Mover Lead Live
     and tap Done.
  6. Add it to the home screen: tap the share button at the bottom (the
     square with an arrow), or the name at the top, then tap
     "Add to Home Screen".
  7. On the next screen you can change the name under the icon and tap the
     icon to pick a color and symbol, or a photo. Tap Add at the top right.
  8. Tap Done to close Shortcuts. "Mover Lead Live" is now on your home
     screen.
  Optional tidy-up: press and hold the plain Bluefy icon, tap Remove App >
  Remove from Home Screen. This only hides it. Bluefy stays installed, and
  your new button still works.

 Note: iPhone can't make a home-screen icon that opens a page directly
 inside Bluefy. "Mover Lead Live" opens Bluefy; from there tap Mover Lead in
 your Favorites if it isn't already showing.

 YOUR TWO BUTTONS
  - Mover Lead (your existing home-screen icon, runs in Safari): everyday
    use and typing in Kestrel numbers. Works with no signal.
  - Mover Lead Live (opens Bluefy): when the Calypso is out. Needs a signal
    to open (see below).


NO SIGNAL AT THE RANGE? READ THIS
 Bluefy does NOT load Mover Lead without a signal. Safari saves Mover Lead on
 your phone for offline use; Bluefy can't. If you open Mover Lead Live with no
 signal, or Bluefy gets closed and has to reload the page, you'll get an error
 or a blank page.

 What still works with no signal, once the page is open:
  - The Bluetooth link to the Calypso (it's phone-to-meter, no internet).
  - All the hold calculations.

 Routine for a range with no signal:
  1. Open Mover Lead Live and let Mover Lead load BEFORE you lose signal
     (at home, on the drive, in the parking lot).
  2. Connect the Calypso and check your settings while you still have signal.
  3. Leave Bluefy open. Don't swipe it closed, and switch to other apps as
     little as possible. Locking the phone is usually fine.
  4. Backup: your regular Mover Lead icon (Safari) works fully offline. If
     Bluefy loses the page, use it and type the wind in by hand.

 Test it at home: load Mover Lead in Bluefy, turn on Airplane Mode, swipe
 Bluefy closed and reopen it. You'll see what happens at the range.


AT THE LINE
 1. Set the Calypso on the firing line with its ORIENTATION MARK POINTING AT
    THE TARGET (downrange). The app reads wind relative to that mark, so this
    matters.
 2. Tap Mover Lead Live on your home screen (or open Mover Lead in Bluefy)
    and tap CONNECT CALYPSO.
 3. Pick your meter from the list. It usually shows as "ULTRASONIC".
 4. Leave LIVE WIND ticked. The wind fields fill in and the hold updates
    about once a second.

 The readout looks like this:
   6.7 mph from 10 o'clock - crosswind 5.8 mph L -> R
   10-second average - battery 80% - filling wind fields

 - "from 10 o'clock" is where the wind is coming from. 12 o'clock is the
   target, 3 o'clock is your right.
 - "crosswind" is the part of the wind blowing across your line of fire. That
   is what goes into the wind field. Wind straight from 12 or 6 o'clock gives
   little or no crosswind.
 - The number is a 10-second average, so single gusts don't swing your hold.


TAKING OVER BY HAND
 - Typing a wind speed or tapping L -> R / R -> L turns Live wind off, so your
   entry sticks. The meter keeps showing its reading.
 - Tick LIVE WIND again to hand control back to the meter.
 - Tap DISCONNECT when you're done. If the meter drops off, the wind fields
   keep the last reading.


TROUBLESHOOTING
 - The box says Safari doesn't have Bluetooth: you're in Safari or the
   regular Mover Lead home-screen icon. Use Mover Lead Live (Bluefy) instead.
 - Bluefy shows an error or a blank page: you probably have no signal.
   Bluefy can't open Mover Lead offline (see NO SIGNAL AT THE RANGE). Use
   the regular Mover Lead icon instead. With a signal, check the address
   is exactly edgeleadconversioncalculator.vercel.app.
 - Tapping Connect does nothing or says Bluetooth isn't available: check
   Settings > Privacy & Security > Bluetooth and make sure Bluefy is on, and
   that Bluetooth is on in Control Center.
 - The meter isn't in the list: make sure it's on, charged and within a few
   yards, and that no other app (such as Anemotracker) is connected to it.
   Close that app, then try again.
 - Won't connect, or keeps dropping: switch the meter off and on, then
   connect again.
 - Direction looks reversed (wind from your right shows L -> R): check that
   the orientation mark points at the target. If it does, the meter is
   reporting differently than expected. Open METER DETAILS, take a
   screenshot, and send it in so the app can be adjusted.
 - Battery shows orange: 20% or less. Calypso meters slow down or stop
   sending data when the battery is low.


GOOD TO KNOW
 - Live wind assumes a full-value crosswind at the line. Wind downrange can be
   different. Watch your mirage and impacts, and override when the meter and
   the target disagree.
 - The meter's temperature reading is not used. It reads high in the sun.
   Use Get conditions or type temperature in.
 - Kestrel meters can't connect. Kestrel's Bluetooth link is closed to outside
   apps. Read the Kestrel and type the numbers in. The pressure field takes
   Kestrel station pressure directly.
 - Holds are starting points. Confirm them on a real mover and write the
   confirmed numbers on your arm card.
